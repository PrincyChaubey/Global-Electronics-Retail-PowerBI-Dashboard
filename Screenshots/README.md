# Power BI Sales Performance Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This Power BI project analyzes sales performance across different years,
countries, states, products, brands, customers, pricing segments, and
sales channels.

The dashboard is designed to provide an executive-level view of business
performance and identify key areas for growth and improvement.

 Objectives

- Analyze overall business performance year over year
- Identify top-performing countries and states
- Evaluate product and category performance
- Identify products that require business attention
- Analyze the relationship between product price and units sold
- Identify brands generating the highest revenue
- Compare Online vs. Store sales performance
- Identify key business opportunities and trends

## 💡 Key Insights

- Revenue peaked at approximately **$18.3M in 2019**.
- **Computers** are the strongest category, generating approximately
  **$19M in revenue and $11M in profit**.
- The **$0–$100 price band** generates the highest sales volume,
  exceeding **70K units sold**.
- **Adventure Works** is the leading brand with approximately
  **$12M in revenue**.
- The **United States** contributes approximately **$30M in revenue**
  and around **5.7K customers**.
- **Store sales significantly outperform Online sales**, generating
  approximately **$44M vs. $11M** in revenue.

## 📈 Business Recommendations

- Expand high-performing and high-profit computer products.
- Investigate high-volume products with comparatively low margins.
- Review underperforming products for pricing, promotion, or portfolio decisions.
- Explore growth opportunities in non-US markets.
- Identify strategies to improve Online sales performance.
- Further analyze the relationship between product price and units sold.
